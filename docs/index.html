Why Sovereign?

Built for power users who care about privacy.

Zero-Cost Memory

Written in Rust. Runs in the background using < 1% CPU and minimal RAM. Unlike Python alternatives, it won't drain your battery.

Local-First AI

Uses local Tesseract OCR and on-device vector embeddings. No data ever leaves your machine. No API keys required.

Smart Blacklist

Automatically pauses recording when you open Incognito windows, Bitwarden, or 1Password. You stay in control.

Privacy First

Your data never leaves your device. Ever.

100% Local Processing

All OCR and AI processing happens on your device. Zero network requests after initial setup. No cloud, no telemetry, no data collection.

Automatic Privacy Guards

Intelligently skips recording when you open password managers, incognito windows, or privacy tools. No configuration needed.

Auto-Cleanup

Automatically deletes all data older than 14 days. No manual cleanup required. Your privacy is protected by default.

Important Security Notice

Screenshots are stored unencrypted on your local disk. Anyone with access to your computer can view them. Use at your own risk and ensure your device is properly secured. See our Security Policy for details.
